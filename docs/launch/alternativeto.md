## Description

Funzzle is a free, independent puzzle site with 20 games that run in any phone or desktop browser, with no app and no account.

Every day there is a new Daily Set (Sudoku, Klondike Solitaire, a five-letter word game and a mini crossword) that everyone plays, plus a daily puzzle in each of the other games and an archive of past days. Before a daily puzzle goes live it is checked by a solver: Solitaire, FreeCell and Spider deals have a known winning line, Sudoku and Kakuro grids have exactly one solution, Minesweeper boards never require a guess, and every Mahjong deal can be cleared.

When you're stuck, the coach shows the next logical step in three stages (where to look, why it works, then the answer) and names the technique. Streaks have freezes, you can challenge a friend to the exact puzzle you played, and private leagues work with an invite code.

Funzzle is supported by ads, which don't appear on the game screen on phones; on desktop they sit beside the board and on the result screen.

Funzzle is not affiliated with The New York Times or any of the products listed as alternatives.
