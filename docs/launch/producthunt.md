## Maker's first comment

> Hi Product Hunt, I'm Rundong and I made Funzzle on my own.
>
> [Optional: one or two sentences, in your own words, on why you started it. Don't leave this placeholder in.]
>
> Funzzle is built around a few things I wanted from a puzzle site:
>
> - **No account.** Open it and play. Your streak and stats stay on your device.
> - **Fair puzzles.** Every daily deal or grid is checked by a solver before it goes live: Solitaire, FreeCell and Spider deals have a known winning line, Sudoku and Kakuro have exactly one solution, Minesweeper never needs a guess.
> - **A coach, not a cheat button.** Hints go in steps: where to look, why it works, then the answer, with the technique named (naked single, X-Wing, and so on).
> - **Nothing interrupts a game.** On phones there are no ads on the game screen at all; on desktop there are ads in the side margins and one on the result screen. That's how it stays free.
>
> There are 20 games: Sudoku, Klondike, Spider, FreeCell, Minesweeper, Mahjong, a Wordle-style word game (with four-board, geography and equation modes), a mini and a full crossword, Groups, Spelling Hive, Letter Box, Word Hunt, Word Search, 2048, Block puzzle, Jigsaw, Nonogram, Kakuro and chess puzzles. Daily puzzles have an archive, streaks have freezes so one missed day doesn't reset you, and you can send a friend the exact puzzle you just played or start a private league with an invite code.
>
> It's early and small, so I'd really like to hear what feels off: a puzzle that seemed unfair, a hint that didn't make sense, a game that's awkward on your phone.
