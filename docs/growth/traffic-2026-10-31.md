# funzzle.com traffic report: 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-30 (14 days, UTC)

Written 2026-10-31. Read-only pull from Cloudflare (Web Analytics/RUM, zone HTTP analytics, Workers, Analytics Engine `funzzle_events`), D1 `funzzle-db`, and Google Search Console (`sc-domain:funzzle.com`).

## TL;DR

- **About 7 human visitors a day.** Analytics Engine counted 54 visits from 48 browsers between 2026-10-24 and 2026-10-30. Some of those are the owner or test runs from Canada.
- **Almost none of them come from search, AI or social.** 45 of the 54 visits (83%) were "direct" and 7 came from search engines. AI assistants sent 0 and social sites sent 0. Search Console shows **6 clicks and 145 impressions in 14 days**, and 4 of those clicks were for the brand name "funzzle".
- **Almost all requests are bots.** Of 64,141 HTML requests to the zone, 14,413 came from verified bots and most of the other 49,728 also look like bots: no or unknown user agent, curl, or headless Linux Chrome. Only about 2,200 were from mobile or Safari browsers.
- **Of the people who arrive, few play and fewer come back.** 14 of the 48 browsers with a visit event started a game. Completions were 32 out of 100 game starts. 3 of 45 new visitors came back the next day. 0 shares, 0 challenges and 0 leagues.
- **Diagnosis: the main problem is that nobody arrives.** People also leave, but with about 50 humans a week the retention numbers are mostly noise. Search demand is 10 impressions a day. Better retention cannot help until arrivals are 10 to 100 times higher.

## How humans are separated from bots

There is no Bot Management on this zone: `botScore` and `clientRefererHost` are not available on its plan. This report therefore uses three sources, ordered from most to least trustworthy for humans:

1. **Analytics Engine `visit` event**, the main human count. It is sent by client-side JavaScript (`trackVisit()` in `lib/games/kit/analytics.ts`, called from `components/Header.tsx`) once per browser per UTC day, and only after hydration. Crawlers that don't run JS never send it. The event also needs a valid UUID clientId stored in localStorage. Headless scrapers that do run JS can still send it, but none showed up as an obvious burst apart from the test sweep described below.
2. **Cloudflare Web Analytics (RUM beacon)**, site tag `a5d21969…`, auto-installed since 2026-10-16. It is also JS-only, but it is sampled at about 10%: every count comes in steps of 10. At this traffic level that makes it a rough cross-check only.
3. **Zone `httpRequestsAdaptiveGroups`**: eyeball HTML requests, split by `verifiedBotCategory` and `userAgentBrowser`. This shows how much of the zone traffic is bots. It is not a human count.

## 8. Data gaps and caveats

- **Analytics Engine only started 2026-10-24 11:52 UTC.** Events, sources, games and returns therefore cover 6.5 of the 14 days. D1 `game_results` also only starts on 10-24.
- **`visit` is sent only from `components/Header.tsx`.** The `/play/*` game pages don't render that Header. Someone who lands directly on a game and leaves never fires `visit`. 24 of 72 clientIds have game events but no visit (Israel, Ecuador, Korea, US). Human visitors are therefore undercounted, probably by a third or more. Source attribution for game-page landers is lost too, since referral is only recorded on `visit`. Even counting all 72 clientIds, traffic is about 10 browsers a day.
- **Owner and test traffic is not filtered.** Canada accounts for 44 events from 22 clientIds, including the automated 10-game sweep on 10-24.
- **Web Analytics is about 10% sampled.** Every value is a multiple of 10, so at about 30 page loads a day a single sample swings a day by 10.
- **No bot score** on this zone plan, and no referer-host dimension in zone analytics. Bot separation therefore relies on JS-only beacons plus Cloudflare's verified-bot list.
- **Search Console lags about 3 days.** The daily rows add up to 7 clicks while the property total says 6, because Google anonymizes rare queries.
- **Referral classes:** "direct" includes stripped referrers. `lib/referral.ts` has no rule yet for other AI hosts (e.g. `chat.deepseek.com`, `you.com`); they would land in "other", which had 2 visits.

## 9. Diagnosis: nobody arrives (mainly), and the few who do mostly leave

**Arrival is the bottleneck, by orders of magnitude:**
- About 7 JS-confirmed human visits a day, roughly 10 if game-page landers are added. A good part of that is one habitual US player, one Israeli player, and the owner.
- 145 search impressions and about 2 non-brand clicks in two weeks. Average position is 27. The long-tail SEO pages sit at positions 30–100 and earn no clicks.
- AI referrals 0, social 0, email and push 0 new.
- Meanwhile the site serves 2–10k HTML pages a day to crawlers. The content is crawled but not ranked.

**Leaving is real, but there is too little data to tune it:**
- 29% of visit-browsers start a game (14 of 48), and about a third of starts are completed.
- D1 return is about 7% (3 of 45). There were 5 repeat browsers in total.
- 0 shares and 0 challenges, so there is no word-of-mouth loop.
- With about 50 people a week, each of these rates has an error bar of ±10–15 points. Optimising them would be tuning noise.

**What follows from this:**
- The next lever is acquisition: getting search impressions to grow (indexing and ranking for the answer and clue pages), plus at least one deliberate channel (Reddit/X posts, the daily email, shareable results).
- Two measurement fixes would make the next report trustworthy:
  - fire `trackVisit()` on `/play/*` pages too;
  - exclude owner and test clientIds.
- After that, judge retention once weekly human visits reach the hundreds.
